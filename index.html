About

I am a Ph.D. student at NETLAB, Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Saewoong Bahk. My research addresses the design of physical and medium-access layers for low-power wireless networks.

From spring through summer 2026, I visited Stanford's S4 Lab as a visiting scholar, hosted by Prof. Zerina Kapetanovic.

Research Interests

Signal processing, physical-layer and medium-access-layer design for low-power wireless networks.
