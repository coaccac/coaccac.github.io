Geonhee Lee

Ph.D. student in wireless networks at Seoul National University.

I work on the lower layers of wireless networks — mostly LoRa, UWB, and 802.15.4 — at NETLAB under Prof. Saewoong Bahk. I work on PHY and MAC design for low-power radios — things like throughput, reliability, and reach for LoRa, UWB, and 802.15.4e (Zigbee).

Before SNU I studied EE at Yonsei. For spring–summer 2026 I'm a visiting scholar at Stanford's S4 Lab, working with Prof. Zerina Kapetanovic.

What I work on

Currently exploring LoRa backscatter and thermal-noise-based communication. Previously worked on PHY/MAC for LoRa, UWB, and Zigbee (802.15.4e) — and broadly interested in anything around low-power wireless.
